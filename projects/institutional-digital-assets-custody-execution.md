# Institutional Digital Assets - Custody-to-Execution Transformation

>
> **Timeline alignment:** The [public FinTech Product Management resume](https://github.com/beastofbayarea/shivam-singh-fintech-product/blob/main/shivam-singh-fintech-product.pdf) is used only to place this case within the D. E. Shaw role dated July 2016-December 2019.

## Evidence-grounded premise

The CFTC's 2017 virtual-currency primer identified operational, cybersecurity, speculative, fraud, and manipulation risks. NISTIR 8202 explained blockchain architecture, consensus, keys, smart contracts, limitations, and the continued need for trust. These sources support an institutional product model that joins custody, authorization, venue risk, execution, reconciliation, and oversight.

## Case approach

- Map the asset lifecycle from onboarding and key control through order, execution, settlement, reconciliation, and reporting.
- Separate custody authority from trading authority and require multi-party controls for material transfers.
- Diligence venues, liquidity, manipulation exposure, cybersecurity, forks, and operational recovery.
- Reconcile on-chain, venue, custodian, and internal records with owned exceptions.

## Evidence-based success measures

Use authorization exceptions, settlement breaks, reconciliation aging, venue concentration, recovery-test results, key-control findings, and loss exposure. These are proposed measures, not historical results.
