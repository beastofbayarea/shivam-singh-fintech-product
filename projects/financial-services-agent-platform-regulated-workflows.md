# Financial-Services Agent Platform - Regulated Workflows

> **Portfolio lens:** FinTech product management, regulated workflows, permissioned agent actions, auditability, human approval, model risk, and platform economics.

## Executive snapshot

Defined a managed execution layer that moved financial-services agents from prototypes into regulated production workflows. The product combined managed reasoning, integrated knowledge, permissioned actions, configurable traces, circuit breakers, human approval, and cost-aware model routing.

## Resume-ready impact

- Led product strategy for a governed agent platform that reduced deployment time 40% and compressed selected pilots from eight weeks to three days.
- Prioritized an integrated knowledge layer that reduced RAG setup from days to under five minutes while preserving controlled access to private financial-services data.
- Introduced traceability, execution limits, approval checkpoints, and model routing that lowered routine-query cost up to 50% and generated roughly $12M in annualized savings.

## Interview story

### Situation

Financial-services customers could demonstrate agent intelligence but stalled on orchestration, permissions, auditability, runaway execution, private-data access, and unit economics. Missing traceability alone could add six to eight weeks to compliance review.

### Task

Turn a generic conversational capability into a secure, observable, bounded, and economically sustainable workflow product for regulated operations.

### Actions

- Mapped production blockers to explicit platform requirements rather than adding more model features.
- Selected managed retrieval as the default journey, prioritizing time-to-first-grounded-agent while preserving controlled access to private data.
- Made trace depth configurable by environment and risk, accepting targeted latency overhead where auditability created greater value.
- Classified work by complexity and risk so routine requests used lower-cost models and high-stakes work used advanced reasoning plus human checkpoints.
- Defined execution limits, approval gates, and circuit-breaker behavior as product capabilities for high-risk workflows.

### Results

- Deployment time improved 40%, with selected pilots moving from eight weeks to three days.
- Knowledge-base setup fell below five minutes.
- Routine-query costs fell by as much as 50%, supporting roughly $12M in annualized savings.
- Beachhead workflows reduced legacy-modernization effort nearly 80%, made mortgage-agent users three times more likely to close, and saved more than 250,000 support hours annually.

## Decisions and trade-offs

- Bundle a managed knowledge layer while retaining controlled flexibility for advanced teams.
- Accept configurable tracing overhead to remove a much larger compliance delay.
- Measure regulated workflow outcomes rather than prototype count.

## Leadership signal

Aligned platform engineering, risk, compliance, application teams, and financial-services customers around one product thesis: governed action, not better chat, was the path to production.
