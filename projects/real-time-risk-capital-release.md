## Evidence-grounded premise

BCBS 239 requires accurate, complete, timely, and adaptable risk-data aggregation. The Basel Committee's 2016 market-risk framework shifted toward expected shortfall under stress and incorporated liquidity horizons. These sources support a real-time risk product that prioritizes data lineage, stress-sensitive exposure, completeness, and governed decision limits.

## Case approach

- Define authoritative positions, prices, risk factors, hierarchies, and ownership.
- Reconcile source and aggregate exposures continuously and quarantine incomplete data.
- Pair intraday measures with stress, liquidity, and non-modellable-risk views.
- Release capital or trading capacity only through governed limits and traceable approvals.

## Evidence-based success measures

Use data timeliness, completeness, reconciliation breaks, stress coverage, limit breaches, decision latency, and unexplained capital variance. These are proposed measures, not historical results.
