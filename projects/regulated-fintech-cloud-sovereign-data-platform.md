## Evidence-grounded premise

NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5 integrates security, privacy, supply-chain risk, governance, and accountability controls. NIST Zero Trust Architecture shifts protection from network location to per-resource, least-privilege decisions. Together they support sovereignty as a verifiable operating model for data location, identities, administrative access, evidence, recovery, and third parties.

## Case approach

- Translate jurisdictional obligations into data, identity, key, logging, support, and recovery requirements.
- Enforce least privilege for users, services, administrators, and vendors.
- Make data flows, control inheritance, exceptions, and evidence visible to regulated customers.
- Test failover, support access, incident response, and exit before production approval.

## Evidence-based success measures

Use control coverage, unauthorized access, data-location exceptions, evidence freshness, recovery testing, third-party findings, and remediation aging. These are proposed measures, not historical results.
